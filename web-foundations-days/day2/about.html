About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple web app for jotting down short notes the moment they come to mind. It keeps your ideas, reminders and to-dos in one place so nothing gets forgotten.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the Home page.
  2. Type your idea into the "New note" field.
  3. Click the "Add Note" button.
  4. Find your new note in the "Your notes" list.

Features

  • Add notes in seconds
  • See a running count of your notes
  • Simple, distraction-free layout
  • Works in any modern browser

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Enter Add the note you have typed
Tab Move to the next field or button
Shift + Tab Move to the previous field or button
